Analogies · Block B

Use, function and measurement

Choose the pair that shows the same relationship as the pair in capitals.

Use, function and measurement

  • What it is: the first word is a thing; the second is what it is for, what it measures, or who uses it.
  • Three connector sentences: ___ is used to ___ (binoculars → observe); ___ measures ___ (thermometer → temperature); ___ uses a ___ (carpenter → hammer). Decide which of the three fits the capitals first.
  • Grammar is a clue: if the second word in capitals is a verb, the answer’s second word must be a verb (ballast → stabilize); if it is a quantity noun, the answer needs a quantity noun (barometer → pressure), not an adjective (ruler → long).
  • Not the same as: a place where it is used (microscope → laboratory), a material it works on (shovel → soil), what it is made of (lens → glass), or a quality it has (ruler → long).

Words to know

  • Instrument words: instrument, implement, tool, utensil, device, apparatus, gauge, meter; calibrate (set an instrument to measure correctly).
  • Instruments and what they measure: barometer → pressure; thermometer → temperature; speedometer → speed; scale → weight (mass); odometer → distance; compass → direction; tracking device → location.
  • Tricky words: ballast (heavy material carried to keep a ship steady: used to stabilize); lubricant (reduces friction; the pair is thing → what it reduces, not thing → action); brake (used to slow, not to stop a vehicle completely); filter → water (the material, not the action: purify); compass → sailor (user, in the wrong direction).
