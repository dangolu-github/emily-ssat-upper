Court and Castle

Seventeen words for the people around a throne and for how a reign is held, given up or taken.

1

Seventeen words

Around the throne

jester

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

a performer kept at a court to keep the monarch and the guests merry with tricks, songs and daring talk

The duke’s jester could tease his master about matters no minister dared to mention.

herald

n., v.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

n. an official who carried a monarch’s messages and read them out in public · v. to be a sign that something is coming

A herald rode ahead to announce that the prince would arrive by nightfall. · Dark clouds heralded the storm.

valet

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

a man’s own servant, who sees to his wardrobe and his daily needs

The earl’s valet laid out his master’s coat and boots before dawn.

courier

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

a messenger who carries messages or packages, usually in haste

A courier left the palace at midnight with a sealed message for the northern army.

sentry

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

a man posted at a gate or on a wall to keep a lookout and stop strangers

The sentry at the north gate challenged every rider who came by night.

entourage

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

the attendants and followers who go about with a great noble or a monarch

The duchess arrived with an entourage of thirty riders, cooks and maids.

Rank and reign

monarch

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

one who reigns over a country by right of birth, such as an emperor or an empress

The young monarch was crowned at sixteen and reigned for sixty years.

sovereign

n., adj.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

n. a monarch · adj. self-governing, with no one above it

The two sovereigns met at the river. · A sovereign country answers to no other.

heir

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

the one who will receive a throne, a rank or an estate when its holder dies

With no child of her own, the duchess named her nephew as her heir.

noble

n., adj.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

n. one of high rank by birth, such as a duke, an earl or a baron · adj. of high rank; also: fine in character

The nobles of the north refused to send their sons to court.

reign

v., n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

to hold the throne as a monarch · the years during which a monarch holds the throne

During her long reign the country saw three new cathedrals rise.

realm

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

a country under a monarch; also used more widely, as in the realm of ideas

Messengers carried the news to every corner of the realm.

decree

n., v.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

an official order made by a monarch or a government, which must be obeyed as law · to order in this way

By the emperor’s decree, every gate in the realm was shut at sunset.

abdicate

v.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

to step down from a throne or a high position of one’s own free will

Old and tired, the emperor abdicated in favor of his daughter.

usurp

v.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

to take a throne or a position with no right to it

The duke usurped the throne while the rightful heir was still a child.

homage

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

honor paid to another in public; in the Middle Ages, a noble’s public pledge to serve his lord

Each baron knelt in turn to pay homage to the new monarch.

treason

n.
Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

the act of betraying one’s own country or monarch

Selling the plans of the fortress to a rival monarch was treason.
