Five words: effort and endurance.

01 Effort and endurance

sustain v.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

To keep something going over time; to support or maintain it.

No one understood how a four-ounce bird could sustain a journey of forty thousand miles without collapsing from exhaustion.

expend v.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

To use up energy, effort, or resources.

Because the terns ride the wind, they expend far less energy on their winding route than a straight flight would demand.

grueling adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Extremely tiring and demanding.

The grueling climb to the ridge left the researchers too exhausted to record their observations that evening.

persist v.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

To continue firmly despite difficulty or opposition.

Although the chimpanzees fled whenever she approached, the scientist persisted, watching them through binoculars for months until they accepted her presence.

longevity n.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Long life; the length of time something lasts.

The tern's longevity surprised the scientists: a bird that flies so far each year might have been expected to wear out quickly.
