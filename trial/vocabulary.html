01 How believable is it?

plausible adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Seeming reasonable or likely to be true.

Although the guide offered a plausible explanation for the missing inscription, no surviving document showed that the inscription had ever existed.

credible adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Believable or worthy of trust.

The witness gave a credible account of the journey: several unfamiliar names she mentioned also appeared in records discovered years later.

credulous adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Too ready to believe something without enough evidence.

The credulous collector accepted the dramatic story about the painting, even though the seller could produce no record of its previous owners.

authentic adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

Genuine; actually what it is claimed to be.

The letter was authentic, but its writer had not witnessed the battle, so the historian still questioned the accuracy of its description.

spurious adj.

Meaning & exampleHide meaning & example

False or not genuine, despite appearing valid or real.

The apparently ancient map proved spurious when an examiner discovered that its paper contained a material introduced only in the twentieth century.
